Privacy Policy

Veloqube: Highway Rush
Developer: Summer B Games
Last updated: November 2, 2026

Summer B Games provides Veloqube: Highway Rush, a mobile arcade racing game. This Privacy Policy describes how the current version handles information.

1. Personal information

The current version does not require account registration or sign-in and does not ask players to submit personal information. We have not integrated third-party advertising networks, analytics services, or account login services into the game.

2. Local game data

The game saves high scores, virtual coins, unlocks, and game progress locally on the player's device to maintain progress between sessions. This gameplay data is not sent to Summer B Games by the game.

3. Advertising and in-app purchases

The current version does not show third-party advertisements or offer purchases using real money. Any future introduction of these features will require an update to this policy and applicable store disclosures.

4. Children's privacy

The game does not ask children to provide personal information and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through its current gameplay features. We will review applicable children's privacy requirements before introducing features that process personal information.

5. Data retention and deletion

Local gameplay data remains on the device until removed by the player or operating system. Clearing the application's storage or uninstalling the game may erase saved progress. We do not maintain a server-side account or gameplay history for players.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when game functionality or legal requirements change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised date.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact Summer B Games at summerbstorellc@gmail.com.
